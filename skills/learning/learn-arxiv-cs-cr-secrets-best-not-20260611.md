## 内容摘要

arXiv:2606.10097v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Attackers often identify DNS traffic to disrupt or compromise Internet services. While prior work has focused on encrypting queries using DNS over TLS, HTTPS, or QUIC to counter such attacks, we consider IETF protocols designed for resource-constrained IoT devices and empirically analyze the potential of obfuscating DNS traffic in addition to encryption. We create a dataset of machine-to-machine-compatible data objects along with the corresponding DNS resolution processes, evaluating 296 deployment scenarios of resolving host names, including DNS over the Constrained Application Layer Protocol (CoAP) and an onion routing flavor of CoAP under varying link-layer conditions.
